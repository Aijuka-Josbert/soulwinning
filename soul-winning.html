Winning Souls.
Making Disciples.

Redeeming souls from the world, discipling them, and making disciples of Christ.

“Jesus came and told his disciples, “I have been given all authority in heaven and on earth. Therefore, go and make disciples of all the nations, baptizing them in the name of the Father and the Son and the Holy Spirit.
Teach these new disciples to obey all the commands I have given you. And be sure of this: I am with you always, even to the end of the age.”
— Matthew 28:18–20

The Soul-Winning Journey

1

Reach

Going to the highways and byways—urban centers, villages, and homes—to proclaim the gospel of Jesus Christ.

2

Welcome

Receiving new believers with love, providing a warm church family where they are accepted and cared for.

3

Disciple

Teaching obedience to all Christ commanded, grounding believers in Scripture and prayer.

4

Grow

Nurturing spiritual maturity through worship, fellowship, and accountability in Home Cells.

5

Send

Equipping and releasing every disciple to reach others, continuing the cycle of multiplication.

Evangelism & Outreach

Urban Evangelism

Taking the gospel to the towns and trading centers of Wakiso District, reaching traders, workers, and families.

Village Evangelism

Visiting rural communities, sharing Christ in homes, and planting seeds of faith in every village.

Charity & Sanitation

Demonstrating the love of Christ through feeding, clothing, and community cleaning initiatives.

Home Cells & Prayer

Discipleship happens in small groups and prayer meetings where believers are nurtured and equipped.

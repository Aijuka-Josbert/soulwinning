About Us

Knowing Christ, pursuing eternity, sharing the gospel—since 2012.

Our History

25 July 2012 – in Kireka, a small gathering began. Pastor Ntumwa Justin, three members, and a simple drum. That was the humble beginning of Soul Winning Mission Church.

For nearly nine years, the church grew in faith, in numbers, and in love for the community. In 2021, God opened a new chapter: the church moved to its current location in Mbalwa, Wakiso District, beside Hosanah Primary School.

Today, we continue to develop our church building and our ministry, always with the same mission: to win souls, make disciples, and glorify Christ.

Our Vision

To see every soul in Mbalwa and beyond redeemed, discipled, and sent as a witness for Christ.

Our Mission

To proclaim the gospel, build authentic community, and equip believers to live out their faith in every area of life.

Our Biblical Foundation

“Go therefore and make disciples of all nations, baptizing them in the name of the Father and of the Son and of the Holy Spirit, teaching them to observe all that I have commanded you.”
— Matthew 28:18–20

We are rooted in the authority of Scripture, the centrality of Christ, and the power of the Holy Spirit. Every teaching, every ministry, and every outreach flows from this foundation.

Our Values

Love & Unity – We are one body in Christ, committed to loving one another as He loved us.
Faithfulness – We hold fast to God's Word and His promises.
Prayer – We seek God's face in all things.
Community – We grow together, carry each other's burdens, and rejoice together.

Looking Ahead

We continue to grow in faith, in numbers, and in our ability to serve the community. As we develop our church building and expand our ministries, our heart remains fixed on the Great Commission. We invite you to be part of this journey.

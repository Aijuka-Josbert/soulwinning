Serving Christ and the community through dedicated ministries.

Home Cell

Home Cells

Members meet in designated homes for prayer, worship, fellowship and spiritual growth. These small groups are the heartbeat of our church family, where believers are known, loved, and discipled.

“Where two or three are gathered in my name, there am I among them.”

I have been looking at the information currently available about the church, including the weekly program, location and the church's description.

Before I start designing the website, I would like us to first brainstorm what the church should look and feel like online.

My goal isn't to give you a generic church website where we simply put the church name, a few pictures and contact information. I would like us to create a website that is uniquely Soul Winning Mission Church and can grow with the church.

🌐 The idea for the website

Imagine someone hears about Soul Winning Mission Church for the first time and searches for the church online.

Within a few seconds, they should be able to understand:

Who we are
What we believe
Where we are located
When we meet
What happens at our church
What the church is passionate about
How they can become part of the church
How they can contact or reach us

But more importantly, they should get a feeling of the church, rather than just reading information.

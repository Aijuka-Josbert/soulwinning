A Word of Welcome

Welcome to Soul Winning Mission Church Video coming soon

A Christ-Centered Community

We are a Bible-believing, born-again fellowship in Mbalwa for worship, biblical teaching, prayer, fellowship, and evangelism. Our heart is to know Christ deeply and to make Him known.

From the beginning, this church has been built on the conviction that the gospel changes lives. We gather not for religious routine, but for transformed living through the power of God's Word and the Holy Spirit.

Our Story

What God began in Kireka in 2012 continues to grow in Mbalwa today.

2012

Beginnings in Kireka

On 25 July 2012, Pastor Ntumwa Justin gathered with three members and a simple drum. That small beginning marked the birth of Soul Winning Mission Church.

~2021
Current locationPhoto coming soon

Moving to Mbalwa

After nearly nine years of growth, the church established its current location in Mbalwa, Wakiso District. We continue to develop our building and community presence.

Continuing the Mission

Today, we remain committed to winning souls, making disciples, and serving the community in the love of Christ.

Privacy Policy

How we handle your personal information.

Last updated: August 2026

Soul Winning Mission Church ("we", "us", "our") respects your privacy. This policy explains how we collect, use, and protect any personal information you provide when you use our website or contact us.

Information We Collect

  • Contact forms: Name, email address, phone number, and message content.
  • Usage data: Anonymous analytics (e.g., pages visited) via standard server logs – we do not use third‑party tracking cookies.

How We Use Your Information

  • To respond to your inquiries, prayer requests, or messages.
  • To send you occasional updates about church events (only if you opt‑in).
  • We will never sell, rent, or share your data with third parties for marketing purposes.

Data Security

We implement reasonable security measures to protect your personal data. However, no transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

Your Rights

You may request access, correction, or deletion of your personal data by contacting us at soulwinningmissionchurch1@gmail.com.

Third‑Party Services

Our contact form uses Web3Forms (https://web3forms.com) to process submissions. Their privacy policy applies to data transmitted to them.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be posted here.
